Reptilita is a mobile-first care companion for reptile and amphibian keepers. Track animals, photos, feeding and cleaning schedules, journal events, care history, exports, and optional cloud sync from one calm daily dashboard.

## Full Description

Reptilita helps reptile and amphibian keepers stay on top of daily care without turning husbandry into spreadsheet work.

Build a care library for snakes, lizards, turtles, frogs, and other herps. Add animal profiles, photos, species details, morph notes, feeding preferences, breeding status, schedules, and journal entries. The Today dashboard shows what is due, overdue, or complete so care routines stay visible.

Core features:

- Animal profiles for reptiles and amphibians
- Photo cards and profile history
- Feeding, cleaning, and routine check schedules
- Today and week views for upcoming care
- Overdue catch-up options for repeating reminders
- Journal/event history for feeding, weight, shed, cleaning, health notes, and general observations
- Care insights based on saved profile details and care history
- Breeding and genetics tools for record keeping and planning
- PDF and calendar exports for sharing care records
- Local-first storage on your device
- Optional account sign-in and cloud sync for supported records
- Backup/import tools for moving or restoring local data
- Light, dark, and system appearance modes

Reptilita is designed for record keeping, husbandry organization, and educational care support. It does not provide veterinary diagnosis, treatment, emergency triage, or medication instructions. Photo Health preview and AI-assisted content are informational only and should never replace a licensed reptile-experienced veterinarian.
