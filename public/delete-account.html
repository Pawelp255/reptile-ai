Delete Your Reptilita Account

App: Reptilita · Developer: LimeKing Development · Contact: support@reptilita.com

How to delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open the Reptilita app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom navigation bar).
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm by typing your account email address when prompted.

Deletion runs immediately on our servers. Once confirmed it cannot be undone.

What is deleted

Account deletion permanently removes from our servers:

  • your user account (including your email address and sign-in credentials),
  • all reptile and amphibian profiles,
  • all care schedules and tasks,
  • all journal and care events,
  • all uploaded animal photos,
  • your profile data (display name, avatar, preferences),
  • any public share links you created.

Data stored only on your device (offline records that were never synced) is under your control and can be removed with Settings → Clear all data or by uninstalling the app.

What may be retained, and for how long

  • Encrypted backups: deleted records may persist in our database provider's routine encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten by the normal backup cycle. Backups are not used to restore deleted accounts.
  • Server logs: limited technical logs (for example request or error logs that may reference your account identifier) are retained for up to 30 days for security and fraud prevention, then deleted automatically.
  • Support correspondence: if you emailed support, that correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve your request and to meet legal obligations.

We retain nothing else after deletion, and we never sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.

If you cannot access the app

Email support@reptilita.com from the email address associated with your account and request account deletion. We will verify the request and delete the account and all associated data described above. You will receive a confirmation reply when it is complete.
