Reptilita — Privacy Policy

App: Reptilita · Developer: LimeKing Development · Effective: July 21, 2026 · Contact: support@reptilita.com

Reptilita ("we", "the app"), developed and operated by LimeKing Development, helps you care for reptiles and amphibians. This policy describes how information is handled when you use our web app, PWA, and native (iOS/Android) builds.

What you store locally

Animal profiles, journal entries, care schedules, genetics inputs, backups, and similar data are stored primarily on your device (for example in the browser's IndexedDB or equivalent native storage). This local data stays on the device unless you export it, enable cloud sync, or use features that intentionally send data to our servers.

Account and cloud sync (Supabase)

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, we use Supabase (hosted database, authentication, and file storage) under project infrastructure we operate. Cloud sync stores copies of animals, care schedules, journal entries, and photos you choose to sync, associated with your account identifier. Authentication identifiers (such as your email address) and profile fields you provide are processed to run the service. All traffic between the app and Supabase uses industry-standard transport encryption (HTTPS/TLS).

Photos

Photos are selected through your device's system photo picker only when you choose to add them. The Android app requests no camera or media-library permission. Synced photos are stored in a private storage bucket linked to your account; they are not public and are not shared with third parties.

Optional assistant features

Some builds of Reptilita include an optional care-assistant feature. Where an assistant is available and you choose to use it, parts of your question and optional context you select may be processed by our server-side functions, which may call a third-party model provider to generate a reply for that session only. Assistant features are not included in every build, are never enabled without your action, and are not required to use the app. Do not paste secrets or passwords into any assistant.

Public sharing

Features that create a public link (for example a shareable care card) transmit only the data you explicitly publish. Revoking a link or not creating one limits further exposure.

Analytics and logging

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We may keep limited technical server logs (for example errors or sync status) needed to operate and secure the service. We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with third parties for advertising.

Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all synced data from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. Full details, including what is deleted and how long residual copies may persist, are on our dedicated page: reptilita.com/delete-account.

If you cannot access the app, email support@reptilita.com from your account email address and we will process the deletion for you.

Your choices

You can use the app entirely offline without an account. You can stop cloud processing by signing out or not enabling sync. Clearing local data in Settings removes on-device records but does not automatically erase cloud copies tied to your account; use the deletion path above for that.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating this page and the effective date above.

This summary is for transparency and is not legal advice. For jurisdictional rights (access, correction, erasure), contact support@reptilita.com.
